Why I Am Taking CS-180

I am taking Intro to AI & ML (CS-180) because I want to understand how computers can perform tasks that usually require human intelligence. I am interested in learning how computers can learn from data, recognize patterns, and make decisions.

What I Hope to Gain

Through this course, I hope to gain a strong understanding of artificial intelligence and machine learning. I want to improve my coding and problem-solving skills while learning how AI can be applied to real-world challenges.

I also hope to gain hands-on experience creating AI projects and understand the ethical responsibilities involved in AI development.

My Experience with AI

One specific moment when AI showed up in my life was when I used ChatGPT to help break problems into smaller steps. It helped me explore different ways to solve problems and understand how AI can generate responses based on patterns in data.

This experience made me curious about how artificial intelligence and machine learning work behind the scenes and inspired me to learn more about these technologies.

My Concerns About AI

One concern I have about AI is that it can sometimes provide incorrect or misleading information while sounding confident. I worry that people may rely too heavily on AI without verifying whether the information is accurate.

I believe AI is a useful tool, but it should be used responsibly. People should think critically and verify information whenever possible.

Course Objective I Am Most Curious About

This matters to me because I enjoy programming and solving problems. I want to understand how AI works behind the scenes and use that knowledge to develop useful projects, explore robotics, and prepare for a future career in artificial intelligence and computer science.
