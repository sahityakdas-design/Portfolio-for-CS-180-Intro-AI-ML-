About Me

My name is Sahitya Das, and I am majoring in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. I am taking Intro AI & ML (CS-180) because I want to understand how computers can perform tasks that usually require human intelligence, learn from data, recognize patterns, and make decisions. Through this course, I hope to gain a strong understanding of artificial intelligence and machine learning, improve my coding and problem-solving skills, and learn how AI can be applied to real-world challenges. I also want to gain hands-on experience creating AI projects and understand the ethical responsibilities of AI development. The course objective I am most curious about is understanding how machine learning allows computers to recognize patterns, learn from data, and make decisions. This matters to me because I enjoy programming and solving problems, and I want to understand how AI works behind the scenes. I hope to use this knowledge to develop useful projects, explore robotics, and prepare for a future career in artificial intelligence and computer science.

One specific moment when AI showed up in my life was when I used ChatGPT to help me understand how to solve problems in smaller steps. It helped me explore different ways to solve problems and understand how AI can generate responses based on patterns in data. This experience made me curious about how artificial intelligence and machine learning work behind the scenes and inspired me to learn more about these technologies. One concern I have about AI is that it can sometimes provide incorrect or misleading information while sounding confident. I worry that people may rely too heavily on AI without checking whether its answers are accurate. I believe AI can be a useful tool, but it is important to think critically, verify information, and use it responsibly. Outside of academics, I enjoy playing the keyboard piano in my free time and riding my scooter outdoors during the warm late-spring and summer months. I am also proud to have earned my First Degree Black Belt in karate, which has taught me discipline, dedication, and perseverance.
